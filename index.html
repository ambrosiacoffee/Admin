About Us

OUR STORY

Roasted With Passion, Served With Pride

Ambrosia began with a simple belief — that great coffee starts with great beans, and great beans deserve careful hands. We work directly with small farms to source single-origin coffees and signature blends, roasting each batch in small quantities to preserve its flavour, character, and freshness.

From our roastery to your cup, every step is done with care. For us, coffee isn't simply a drink — it's a craft, shaped by quality beans, thoughtful roasting, and a genuine passion for great coffee.
